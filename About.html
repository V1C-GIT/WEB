About Us

About Us T&T Paparazzi is a South African photography
agency dedicated to capturing moments that tell
meaningful stories. We specialize in professional
photography for events, portraits, celebrations,
brands, and special occasions. Our goal is to capture
more than just photographs — we capture memories,
emotions, and moments that last a lifetime.
With a creative eye and attention to detail,
we work closely with our clients to ensure every image
reflects their personality, vision, and story.
Whether it’s a special event, a personal portrait session,
or professional content for your brand, T&T Paparazzi
is committed to delivering high-quality photography
and an experience you’ll remember.

Our motto: “Capturing moments. Creating memories.”
